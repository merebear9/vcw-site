Vermilion County Watchdog is sharing this alert as a public service.

Tyler Wagaman was last seen in the Mars Hill area on June 15. He is described as 6 feet 2 inches tall, approximately 190 pounds, with blue eyes and a thin build. Those who know him describe him as shy and soft-spoken.

Wagaman was believed to be traveling in a black pickup truck bearing Indiana license plate TK9450XP.

A $7,000 reward is being offered for information leading to Wagaman's safe return.

Anyone with information is asked to call 317-346-6336.
