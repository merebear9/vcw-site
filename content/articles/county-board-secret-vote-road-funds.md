Records obtained by Vermilion County Watchdog through a Freedom of Information Act request show the Vermilion County Board approved a budget amendment shifting **$2.3 million** in road maintenance dollars into a downtown parking garage project — without the item ever appearing on a public meeting agenda.

The amendment, dated March 14, was passed as part of a consent agenda item labeled only "miscellaneous budget transfers." No board member is recorded raising a question about it during the meeting, according to minutes reviewed by this newsroom.

"This is exactly the kind of thing that erodes public trust," said one county employee who spoke on condition of anonymity because they were not authorized to discuss internal budget matters. "People think their road taxes are going to fix potholes, not a parking garage downtown."

## How the Money Moved

According to the FOIA documents obtained by Vermilion County Watchdog, the transfer was initiated by the county's finance office at the request of the Building and Grounds Committee. The committee's own meeting minutes from February make no mention of the road fund as a financing source.

Three separate line items were consolidated and re-designated over a six-week period:

- $900,000 from the FY2026 township road resurfacing allocation
- $850,000 from a bridge inspection reserve fund
- $550,000 from a rural road salt and materials contingency line

County officials did not respond to repeated requests for comment by publication time.

## What Happens Next

Vermilion County Watchdog has filed a follow-up FOIA request seeking all email correspondence between the finance office and Building and Grounds Committee members related to the transfer. We will continue to update this story as records are received.
