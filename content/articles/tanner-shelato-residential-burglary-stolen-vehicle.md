Tanner J. Shelato was booked into the Vermilion County Jail at approximately 4:57 p.m. on June 30 on charges of residential burglary, possession of a stolen motor vehicle, and violation of pretrial release, according to Vermilion County Sheriff's Department booking records.

The residential burglary charge alleges the unlawful entry of a residence with intent to commit a theft or felony. Authorities also allege Shelato was found in possession of a stolen motor vehicle and that he violated the conditions of a previously granted pretrial release.

Current custody records show Shelato remains in the custody of the Vermilion County Sheriff's Department. The case will proceed through the Vermilion County court system.

As with all arrests, these are charges, not convictions. Shelato is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty in a court of law.
