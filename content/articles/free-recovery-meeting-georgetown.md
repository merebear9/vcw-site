A local addiction recovery group is holding free weekly meetings in Georgetown, offering a no-cost space for anyone struggling with alcohol or drug addiction — no registration or fees required.

The Hitchhiker's Guide to Sobriety, a 12-Step AA-based recovery group, meets every Wednesday from 7 to 8 p.m. at the Georgetown Event Center, 109 E. West St. in Georgetown. Coffee is provided, and meetings run year-round regardless of weather.

Addiction rarely affects just one person — it ripples through families, friendships, and the wider community. Organizers say the group's goal is simple: make sure no one has to face recovery alone.

Anyone interested can simply show up; no referral or registration process is required.

*If you or someone you know is struggling with addiction, consider sharing this story — sometimes hope begins with a single meeting.*
