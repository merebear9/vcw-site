A Danville city official submitted a resignation letter effective immediately on Tuesday, one day after Vermilion County Watchdog submitted formal records requests connected to an active municipal ethics board inquiry.

The resignation letter, obtained by this newsroom, cites "personal reasons" and does not reference the ethics inquiry. Sources with direct knowledge of the matter, who spoke on condition of anonymity because the inquiry is ongoing, say the two events are connected.

This is a developing story. Vermilion County Watchdog will update this report as new information is confirmed.

## What We Know So Far

- The resignation was submitted in writing Tuesday morning and took effect immediately.
- The ethics board inquiry was opened several weeks ago; its subject matter has not been made public.
- Vermilion County Watchdog's records requests, submitted Monday, sought communications and financial disclosure filings related to the inquiry.
