Two 19-year-old Danville men are facing first-degree murder charges following the fatal shooting of 24-year-old Krisean M. Watson on Plum Street on July 10.

Erik Jimenez and Javonte Forman were arrested by the Danville Police Department and charged with first-degree murder, aggravated battery on a public way, and mob action. The investigation remains active.

## What Happens Next

Both men are expected to appear in Vermilion County Circuit Court for a hearing on whether they will be held without bond pending trial. Under Illinois' pretrial detention law, the state carries the burden of proving by clear and convincing evidence both that the proof of guilt is strong and that no set of release conditions could reasonably protect public safety. Detention is not automatic, even in a murder case — the court must find the state has met that standard.

It bears repeating that these are charges, not convictions. Under the U.S. and Illinois constitutions, Jimenez and Forman are presumed innocent unless and until guilt is proven beyond a reasonable doubt in court.
