Jamie Harper was 20 years old when she vanished after a party in Rantoul in the early morning hours of March 10, 2007. Nearly two decades later, she has never been found, and her disappearance remains unsolved.

Harper, of Paxton, had attended a party in Rantoul that night and made plans to spend the night at a friend's house. She never arrived. Investigators say she left the party with an acquaintance who returned roughly ninety minutes later without her. That person was brought in for questioning but declined to take a polygraph exam.

In the days that followed, investigators say the man left Illinois, traveling through several other states before he was located and arrested in Florida on unrelated Champaign County warrants and extradited back to Illinois. When investigators later attempted to question him again about Harper's disappearance, he requested an attorney.

No one has ever been charged in connection with Harper's disappearance, and her case remains open.
