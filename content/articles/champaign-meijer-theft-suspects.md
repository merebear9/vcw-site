Champaign County Crime Stoppers is seeking the public's help identifying two suspects in a theft at a Meijer store in Champaign.

According to Crime Stoppers, the two suspects entered the store together, and one created a distraction while the other removed security tags from several items. The pair then left the store without paying for approximately $600 in merchandise. Investigators say the suspects left the area using public transportation.

The suspects are described as two young Black women between 18 and 25 years old, both with bright red hair. The first suspect was seen wearing a black leather jacket, patterned pants, and pink shoes. The second suspect wore a red and black top, distressed jeans, and flip-flops.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Champaign County Crime Stoppers at 217-373-8477, submit a tip online at 373tips.com, or use the P3 Tips mobile app.
