Champaign County Crime Stoppers is asking for the public's help identifying a suspect in a June 12 theft at a Home Depot on Bloomington Road in Champaign.

According to Crime Stoppers, the suspect selected plumbing products and multiple carbon monoxide detectors valued at approximately $4,500, then paid with counterfeit $100 bills.

The suspect is described as a heavy-set Black male, approximately 50 years old, wearing a gray and yellow baseball cap with an American flag patch, a gray work shirt, dark gray work pants, and tan boots. He was reportedly driving a white full-size SUV.

Anyone with information is asked to contact Champaign County Crime Stoppers at 217-373-8477, submit a tip online at 373tips.com, or use the P3 Tips mobile app. Tips may be submitted anonymously and could qualify for a cash reward.
