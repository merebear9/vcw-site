A Jacksonville man has been arrested on a felony charge of aggravated animal cruelty after a neighbor's cat was found beaten to death outside its home.

According to the cat's family, their pet, known as Binx, was found dead outside their home on May 31. After reviewing surveillance footage, the family contacted the Jacksonville Police Department.

Their neighbor, Dustin J. Jones, was arrested and charged with felony aggravated animal cruelty in connection with the incident. Jones has since been released and continues to reside next door to the family.

As with all arrests, this is a charge, not a conviction. Jones is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty in a court of law.
